# 00 — Introduction

Welcome to the Content Workbook. This workbook is where all of the website's
launch content is prepared, reviewed and approved — in one place, before it is
built into the website. Completing it is the single most effective thing your
team can do to keep the project on schedule.

## What the workbook is for

- One home for every piece of launch content: page text, images, documents,
  people, partners and legal wording.
- A clear picture, at any moment, of what is finished and what still needs
  attention.
- A shared agreement on what "done" means, so the website can be built and
  tested with the real content.

## The rules that make it work

1. **Only content marked Approved is built into the website.** Drafting and
   review happen inside the workbook.
2. **The workbook is the authoritative source for launch content.** If text
   exists somewhere else but not here, it is not launch content yet.
3. **One named content lead** coordinates completion; contributors may complete
   assigned sections. **One named decision-maker** (or a defined approval
   group) gives final approval. When an item becomes Approved, the approver's
   name and the approval date are recorded with it.
4. **Credentials are never entered in the workbook.** Access to external
   systems is provided through the project's approved secure method (see the
   Integrations sheet) — never by typing a password into a document.
5. **Changing something after approval** returns it to In review, clears the
   approval fields, and records the reason in its revision note. The website is
   built from the latest approved version only.
6. **Two sign-off moments** close the workbook: implementation sign-off (the
   content is complete enough to build from) and launch reconciliation (the
   record of what was actually published). Both are described in section 90.
7. **After the website is published, WordPress is the authoritative home of the
   live content.** The workbook is the launch record, not a second website.
8. **You are never asked twice.** Anything already established during discovery
   arrives prefilled here for confirmation.

## Who does what

| Status set | Owned by | Used on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not started · Draft · In review · Approved · Not required | Your team | Pages and content entries |
| Needed · Proposed · In review · Approved · Not required | Your team | Assets (images, documents, media) |
| Identified · Account pending · Access pending · Configured · Tested · Ready · Not required | Developer | Integrations |
| Open · Done · Not applicable | As named per row | Launch checklists |
| Not implemented · Implemented · QA verified · Published | Developer | Delivery tracking (developer columns) |

## How the workbook flows

```text
Prefilled from discovery
  → your team drafts and reviews
  → the decision-maker approves each item
  → implementation sign-off (section 90)
  → the developer builds and populates the websites
  → approved corrections are reconciled back into the workbook
  → launch reconciliation: the record of what was published
```

Each signed-off version is recorded in the version manifest
([workbook-version.md](workbook-version.md)) and frozen as a read-only archive.
